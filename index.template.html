📖 ASHRAE 2021 Psychrometric Glossary & Equations

Reference definitions, units, and governing moist-air formulations implemented in PSYCHRO·ULTRA 3.0.

Moist-Air Properties

🌡
Tdb / DBT Dry-Bulb Temperature Sensible temperature of air measured by a dry, shielded thermometer. SI: °C | IP: °F
💧
Twb / WBT Thermodynamic Wet-Bulb Temperature Temperature of adiabatic saturation where water evaporates into air to 100% RH at constant pressure. SI: °C | IP: °F
❄
Tdp / DPT Dew-Point Temperature Temperature at which moist air reaches saturation (RH = 100%) when cooled at constant W and P. SI: °C | IP: °F
💦
RH / φ Relative Humidity Mole-fraction ratio of partial water vapor pressure Pv to saturation vapor pressure Pws(Tdb). % (0–100)
⚖
W / ω Humidity Ratio (Moisture Content) Mass of water vapor per unit mass of dry air. Internal engine uses kg/kg. SI: g/kg | IP: gr/lb (1 lb = 7000 gr)
⚡
h Specific Enthalpy Total sensible + latent thermal energy per unit mass of dry air (0 °C datum in SI; 0 °F datum in IP). SI: kJ/kg | IP: Btu/lb
📐
v / ρ Specific Volume & Moist-Air Density Volume per unit mass of dry air (v); total moist-air density is ρ = (1 + W) / v. SI: m³/kg, kg/m³ | IP: ft³/lb, lb/ft³
🏔
Patm / Pv Barometric & Vapor Partial Pressure Standard sea-level pressure = 101.325 kPa (14.696 psia), corrected for elevation per ASHRAE Ch. 1. SI: kPa / Pa | IP: psia

HVAC Processes, Comfort & Standards

📊
SHR / SHF Sensible Heat Ratio Ratio of sensible heat transfer to total heat transfer: SHR = Qs / Qt. Dimensionless
🎯
ADP / BPF / CF Apparatus Dew Point & Bypass Factor Effective coil surface saturation temperature (ADP); BPF = (T_out − ADP)/(T_in − ADP); CF = 1 − BPF. °C / °F & Fraction (0–1)
🔥
Qs / Ql / Qt / TR Sensible, Latent, Total Load & Tons Qt = ṁ_da · Δh; Qs = ṁ_da · c_pm · ΔT; Ql = Qt − Qs; 1 Ton (TR) = 3.51685 kW = 12,000 BTU/h. SI: kW | IP: BTU/h, TR
🏠
PMV / PPD ASHRAE 55 / ISO 7730 Thermal Comfort Fanger's Predicted Mean Vote (−3 cold to +3 hot) and Predicted Percentage Dissatisfied (%). Comfort band: |PMV| ≤ 0.5 (PPD ≤ 10%). PMV (−3..+3) | PPD (%)
🖥
TC 9.9 (A1–A4) ASHRAE Data Center Thermal Envelopes Recommended (18–27 °C, −9..15 °C Tdp, ≤60% RH) and Allowable A1–A4 IT equipment classes. ASHRAE TC 9.9 (2021)
🌬
Vbz = Rp·Pz + Ra·Az ASHRAE 62.1 Breathing Zone OA Required outdoor ventilation airflow combining people component (Rp·Pz) and building floor area component (Ra·Az). SI: L/s | IP: CFM

Governing ASHRAE Fundamentals (2021) Equations

Barometric Pressure: P = 101325 · (1 − 2.25577×10⁻⁵ · Z)^5.25588 [Pa, Z in m]
Saturation Pressure (Hyland–Wexler): ln(Pws) = C₈/T + C₉ + C₁₀T + C₁₁T² + C₁₂T³ + C₁₃ln(T) (T ≥ 0 °C)
Humidity Ratio: W = 0.621945 · Pv / (P − Pv) [kg_w / kg_da]
Specific Enthalpy (SI): h = 1.006·T + W·(2501 + 1.86·T) [kJ/kg_da]
Specific Enthalpy (IP, 0 °F datum): h_IP = 0.240·T_F + W·(1061 + 0.444·T_F) [Btu/lb_da]
Specific Volume: v = 0.287042·(T + 273.15)·(1 + 1.607858·W) / P_kPa [m³/kg_da]
Thermodynamic Wet Bulb: W = [(2501 − 2.326·Twb)·Ws(Twb) − 1.006·(T − Twb)] / (2501 + 1.86·T − 4.186·Twb)

▲ About PSYCHRO·ULTRA 3.0

Engineering-grade, zero-dependency ASHRAE 2021 interactive psychrometric studio — compiled into a single self-contained HTML application.

What's New in Ultra 3.0

  • ASHRAE Fundamentals (2021) Physics Engine: Full Hyland–Wexler saturation model, thermodynamic wet-bulb solver, universal any-2-of-8 state solver, and exact SI/IP conversions (true 0 °F enthalpy datum).
  • Dual-Canvas HiDPI Architecture: Cached background chart layer + 60 fps foreground overlay with curved cooling-coil paths, animated flow arrows, enthalpy protractor ruler, and scenario ghost overlays.
  • Thermal Comfort & Specialized Envelopes: Real-time ISO 7730 / ASHRAE 55 Fanger PMV/PPD polygons, Givoni bioclimatic zones, and ASHRAE TC 9.9 data center envelopes.
  • HVAC System Wizards & Climate Studio: 44+ global ASHRAE design cities, 8760-hour TMY/EPW density maps, full AHU cycle builder, ERV, 2-stage evaporative cooling, desiccant wheel, humidifier, ADP/BPF coil analyzer, and ASHRAE 62.1 ventilation calculator.
  • Professional Deliverables: HiDPI PNG, scalable Vector SVG, CSV export, Shareable URL hash, JSON v3 (backward-compatible with v2.6.6), and HTML/DOCX engineering reports.
