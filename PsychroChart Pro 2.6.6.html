ⓘ About PSYCHRO·PRO

Professional-grade ASHRAE psychrometric chart analysis software.

Features

  • Full ASHRAE psychrometric calculations (SI & IP units)
  • Altitude & pressure correction per ASHRAE Fundamentals
  • ASHRAE Standard 55 thermal comfort zone overlays
  • Air mixing with mass/enthalpy balance & lever rule
  • Coil analysis with ADP & bypass factor calculation
  • SHR protractor & process type auto-classification
  • Ventilation load calculator (outdoor air)
  • Psychrometric property table generation
  • Bulk CSV point import & project save/load (JSON)
  • PNG/HTML report export with embedded chart
  • Multiple themes: Pro, Classic, and ASHRAE-authentic

📖 Glossary & Abbreviations

All psychrometric properties and abbreviations used in PSYCHRO·PRO.

Psychrometric Properties

🌡️
Tdb / DBT Dry-Bulb Temperature Temperature of air measured by a standard thermometer, not affected by moisture. SI: °C  |  IP: °F
💧
Twb / WBT Wet-Bulb Temperature Temperature read by a thermometer with a wetted wick; indicates evaporative cooling potential. SI: °C  |  IP: °F
🔽
Tdp / DPT Dew-Point Temperature Temperature at which air becomes saturated and condensation begins. SI: °C  |  IP: °F
💦
RH Relative Humidity Ratio of actual water vapour pressure to saturation pressure at the same temperature. % (0–100)
⚖️
W / ω Humidity Ratio Mass of water vapour per unit mass of dry air. Also called moisture content or mixing ratio. SI: g/kg  |  IP: grains/lb
⚡
h Specific Enthalpy Total energy content of moist air per unit mass of dry air (sensible + latent heat). SI: kJ/kg  |  IP: Btu/lb
📐
v Specific Volume Volume occupied per unit mass of dry air. Inverse of density. SI: m³/kg  |  IP: ft³/lb
🏋️
ρ Density Mass of moist air per unit volume. ρ = 1/v. SI: kg/m³  |  IP: lb/ft³

Process & Analysis Terms

📊
SHR Sensible Heat Ratio Ratio of sensible heat to total heat in a process. SHR = Qs / Qt. Dimensionless (0–1)
🎯
ADP Apparatus Dew Point The saturation point where the coil process line intersects the saturation curve. SI: °C  |  IP: °F
🔄
BPF / BF Bypass Factor Fraction of air that bypasses the coil without being conditioned. Dimensionless (0–1)
🌀
CF Contact Factor Fraction of air effectively in contact with the coil. CF = 1 − BPF. Dimensionless (0–1)
🔥
Qs Sensible Heat Heat that changes temperature without changing moisture content. kW (or Btu/h)
🌫️
Ql Latent Heat Heat that changes moisture content without changing temperature. kW (or Btu/h)
⚡
Qt Total Heat Sum of sensible and latent heat. Qt = Qs + Ql. kW (or Btu/h)
🌬️
OA Outdoor Air Fresh air intake from outside for ventilation requirements. L/s or CFM
🔁
RA / SA Return Air / Supply Air RA is air returning from the conditioned space; SA is conditioned air delivered to the space. L/s or CFM
🌀
Pv / Pws Vapour Pressure / Saturation Vapour Pressure Pv is the partial pressure of water vapour in air. Pws is the maximum Pv at a given temperature. RH = Pv / Pws. Pa or kPa
🏔️
Patm Atmospheric Pressure Barometric pressure at a given altitude. Affects all psychrometric calculations. Standard sea level = 101.325 kPa. kPa or psia
